## Task Summary

### Learnability — ROAS, ACOS, TACOS
- **ROAS** = Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend. Measures return on every rupee of ad spend.
- **ACOS** = Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue × 100. Inverse of ROAS. Measures ad cost as % of ad sales.
- **TACOS** = Ad Spend ÷ Total Revenue × 100. Best long-term metric — includes organic sales. Falls naturally as brand equity builds over time.

### Task 1 — GlowNest Key Findings
- Gross margin: ₹520/unit | Net margin (ad unit): ₹224/unit (28% of SP)
- Monthly profit: **₹10,34,000** at base case
- ACOS: **25%** (healthy) | TACOS: **10.7%** (excellent)
- Break-even ACOS: **53%** — very large safety margin
- Q5 scenario: ACOS worsens → still profitable, organic base cushions impact
- Q6 scenario: Doubling spend is only justified for ranking goals, not margin goals
